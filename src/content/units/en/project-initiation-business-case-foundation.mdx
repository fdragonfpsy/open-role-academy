## Why this matters

Many projects start with an enthusiastic sentence such as "we should finally digitise this". Months later nobody can say whether the project succeeded, because nobody wrote down what it was for. Initiation is the short phase in which you turn an idea into something a decision-maker can say yes or no to.

## What a project is

A project is a goal-directed, one-off undertaking with agreed limits such as time, resources and quality. That is different from daily operations, which repeat. A one-off character is the reason a project needs its own start, goal and end. Many project teachings recommend that goals follow the SMART rules: specific, measurable, accepted, realistic and time-bound.

## Problem, goal, business need

Begin with the present state: what is not working, for whom, and how do we know? Only then state the goal. The planning process in general starts with an awareness of the present state before outcomes are set. A business case builds on the same logic: whenever money or effort is spent, it should support a specific business need.

## What a business case contains

A business case captures the reasoning for starting a project. It can be a long document or a short presentation. A formal one typically covers the background, the expected benefits, the options considered, the expected costs and the risks. It also considers the option of doing nothing, including its costs and risks.

## A one-page case (our own template)

Our own template, one page, six lines:

- Problem: what hurts today, with one fact.
- Goal: one measurable sentence with a date.
- Options: at least two, plus "do nothing".
- Benefit and cost: rough numbers, marked as estimates.
- Risks: the top three.
- Ask: what decision you need, from whom, by when.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. Ana in the office team says: "Timesheets on paper are annoying." Her one-page case reads: Problem, 12 people hand in paper sheets and the payroll clerk retypes them, which costs about two hours every month. Goal, all timesheets are entered digitally by the 3rd working day, from 1 April. Options: spreadsheet template, existing HR tool, do nothing. Risk: people forget to enter data. Ask: the head of finance decides by 15 February. Because the case is short, the decision takes ten minutes.

## Key takeaways

- A project is a one-off undertaking with a goal and limits.
- Describe the present state before you state the goal.
- A business case shows need, options, costs, benefits, risks and the do-nothing option.
- One page and one clear ask beat a long document nobody reads.
