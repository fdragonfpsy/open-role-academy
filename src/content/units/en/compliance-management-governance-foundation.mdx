## Why this matters

Every organisation has to follow laws and also its own rules. Legal & Compliance staff help make that happen in a structured way instead of case by case. This unit is education, not legal advice. It describes concepts for Germany and the EU only, with no section numbers, deadlines or amounts; check the current official texts and involve legal counsel for individual cases.

## What compliance means

The Wikipedia article on regulatory compliance describes compliance as conforming to rules such as laws, regulations, standards and internal policies. The German article on Compliance uses the same idea for companies: acting in line with legal requirements and with voluntary commitments such as a code of conduct. Compliance is therefore wider than "obeying statutes": it also covers the organisation's own policies and values.

## Building blocks of a compliance management system

A compliance management system (CMS) is the set of measures that makes rule-following systematic. Typical building blocks are:

- **Tone from the top:** leaders visibly follow and demand the rules. Without it, written rules are easily ignored.
- **Risk assessment:** identify which rules matter most for the business and where breaches are most likely.
- **Policies and a code of conduct:** put expectations into clear, understandable words. The articles on code of conduct describe it as a document that states principles and expected behaviour.
- **Training and communication:** people can only follow rules they know and understand.
- **Monitoring:** check whether the rules are followed.
- **Reporting channels:** ways to raise concerns safely.
- **Continuous improvement:** learn from incidents and update the system.

## Who does what

Compliance is not only the job of one department. Managers and employees in the business carry day-to-day responsibility, while a compliance function advises, trains and monitors. Details differ between organisations, so look up how your employer divides these roles.

## Where to look

Start with the code of conduct and the policy library. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask your compliance contact or legal department before acting.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. A new employee at a small supplier is offered a large discount by a vendor in exchange for a quick order. The code of conduct says that gifts and benefits must be reported to the compliance contact. The employee does not decide alone: they note the offer, say that they must check the internal rule, and ask the compliance contact. The question is solved by the policy and a named contact, not by personal judgement under time pressure.

## Key takeaways

- Compliance means following laws and also internal rules and commitments.
- A compliance management system turns rule-following into a repeatable process.
- Tone from the top, risk assessment, policies, training, monitoring, reporting channels and improvement are the typical building blocks.
- When unsure, ask before you act.
