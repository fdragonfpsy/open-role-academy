## Why this matters

Advanced practitioners shape the system: they decide how risks are mapped, how the channel earns trust and how cases are handled consistently. Quality is judged by outcomes, not by the existence of a hotline. This unit is education, not legal advice. It describes concepts for Germany and the EU only, with no section numbers, deadlines or amounts; check the current official texts and involve legal counsel for individual cases.

## Risk-based programme

Map where corruption risk concentrates: interactions with public officials, licences and permits, customs, tenders, sales through agents, high-value procurement, donations and sponsoring. For each area assess likelihood and impact, check existing controls and add controls where gaps remain. Proportionality applies: stronger controls for high-risk countries, roles and transaction types, lighter processes elsewhere. Typical controls are approval levels, segregation of duties, due diligence on partners, payment checks, registers and periodic testing.

## Culture and incentives

A programme fails if sales or procurement incentives reward results at any price. Review targets and consequence management with HR and management. Visible and consistent consequences for breaches, regardless of rank, show that the rules are meant seriously. The ethics chapter on employee duties in OpenStax (link only; US-centred) discusses loyalty, financial integrity and the tension around criticising the company and whistleblowing; use it as a prompt for discussion rather than as a statement of German law.

## Trust in the reporting system

A channel is only used if people trust it. Evidence of trust: use rates compared with expectations, the share of reports that lead to action, feedback to reporters, and results of surveys on whether staff would report. Typical problems are fear of retaliation, doubts about impartiality, unclear scope and slow replies. The European Commission page notes that the directive requires protection from retaliation and proper follow-up; the Commission also reports shortcomings in some Member States, which shows that implementation quality varies.

## Balancing interests

Protecting reporters does not mean presuming guilt. The accused person has rights too, such as a fair hearing and confidentiality in the process. Data protection rules apply to the case file: collect only what is needed, limit access and set retention periods. The German article on the HinSchG notes that external offices also handle anonymous reports; whether and how your internal channel handles anonymous reports is a design decision to be checked against the current statute with counsel.

## Investigation quality

Define who may open an investigation, who decides measures and how conflicts of interest within the investigating team are excluded. Keep evidence orderly, document steps and reasons, and separate fact-finding from sanction decisions. Report case statistics, trends and lessons learned to the governing body in aggregated form that protects identities.

## Continuous improvement

Feed lessons from cases back into the risk assessment, policies, training and controls. Test the channel itself, for example with a walk-through of a dummy report, and review it regularly.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. Over two years, a group's channel receives few reports, and almost all concern HR matters. A staff survey shows that many people fear that managers learn who reported. The integrity lead moves the intake to a small independent office, publishes how confidentiality is protected, trains managers on the ban on retaliation and starts giving reporters a status update. In the next year more reports arrive, including procurement concerns. The lead presents the change to the management board as a sign of trust, not of more misconduct.

## Key takeaways

- Map corruption risks by business area and apply proportionate controls.
- Incentives and consistent consequences decide whether the programme is credible.
- Measure trust in the channel, not only its existence.
- Balance reporter protection, rights of the accused and data protection.
- Learn from cases and test the channel regularly.
