## Why this matters

A customer's first sentence is often not their problem. "Your website is broken" may mean a forgotten password, a blocked browser or a real fault. Solving the wrong problem quickly is still a failure.

## A problem is a gap

German sources describe problem solving as removing a problem by deliberate thinking: dissatisfaction with the current state is the starting point, and the aim is a wanted state. The steps on the way are called the problem-solving process. For service this gives two questions: what is the situation now, and what does the customer want instead? Customer support itself covers help with using a product and with troubleshooting, so many contacts are exactly this kind of gap.

## Request and need

The request is what the customer asks for ("refund me"). The need is what they want to achieve ("get my money back, or get a working product by Friday"). OpenStax's knowledge gap describes a company that does not know exactly what customers want, for example through failing to listen to feedback and complaints. In a single contact, you close this gap by asking what the customer is trying to do, and what happens if it is not solved.

## Facts before solutions

Collect only what you need: who is affected, what exactly happens, since when, what has already been tried, any error message, and how urgent it is. Write the facts down in the ticket so nobody has to ask again.

## Five-step cycle

Our own working method. One: understand and restate. Two: clarify facts. Three: decide and offer a solution or the next best step. Four: act and tell the customer what you did. Five: follow up if there is a waiting period, and confirm the problem stayed solved.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. A customer writes: "The printer you sold me does not work." The agent asks what happens when printing and which error appears. The customer says the page stays blank. The agent asks since when and what has changed, and learns that a new ink cartridge was fitted yesterday. The agent offers a step: remove the protective strip from the cartridge. The customer confirms the print works, and the agent notes the solution and asks that they reply if it recurs.

## Key takeaways

- A problem is a gap between the current and the wanted state.
- Separate the stated request from the underlying need.
- Gather facts first, write them in the ticket.
- Use a cycle: understand, clarify, offer, act, follow up.
