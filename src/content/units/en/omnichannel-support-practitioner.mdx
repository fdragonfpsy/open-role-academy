## Why this matters

A customer emails on Monday, calls on Wednesday and writes in the chat on Friday. If each contact starts from zero, the customer feels unheard. Consistency across channels is mostly a matter of expectations and records.

## Response expectations

Responsiveness means promptness and willingness: customers should get quick service without delay. A policy that says only "answer quickly" is read differently by everyone, so define it. Our own approach: set a target per channel, show it where the customer sees it, and keep it. Illustrative targets only, to be set by your company: chat answered within minutes, phone within a few rings, email within one working day, social media within a few hours during opening hours.

## One story across channels

Customer relationship management (CRM) software tracks and stores contacts with company staff, such as phone calls, emails, live chat conversations and service requests. For you, this means: log every contact in the one case record, whatever the channel. Our own rule for a switch: before answering, read the previous notes; at the start, say "I see you wrote to us on Monday about the damaged lamp"; at the end, add a short summary. The customer should never have to tell the story twice.

## From public to private

Because comments on social media cannot be controlled and others can read them, answer publicly in a short, friendly way and move details to a private channel. Ask only for the data you really need, and never in the public thread. Questions about when personal data may be processed are for your data protection officer or legal counsel.

## Tone per channel

Phone allows warmth and quick clarification; chat wants short sentences and fast turns; email wants a clear structure with the answer first. Whatever the channel, the substance of the answer must be the same, only form and length change.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. A customer wrote on Monday that the lamp arrived damaged and was promised a reply by Wednesday. On Wednesday he calls. The agent reads the record, says "I see you wrote about the lamp on Monday; I have your photos", offers a replacement and logs: "Phone, Wed: replacement ordered, tracking by email Thursday." In the chat on Friday the next agent reads this note and gives the tracking status without asking again.

## Key takeaways

- Define concrete response times per channel and keep them.
- One case record for all channels; read it before answering.
- Answer publicly and briefly; move details to private; ask only for needed data.
- The substance stays the same across channels; form and length change.
