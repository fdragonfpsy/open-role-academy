## Why this matters

A campaign without a plan spends money and attention without learning anything. Planning does not need to be heavy: a one-page plan that answers a few questions is already far better than starting with the channel.

## What a campaign is

A campaign is a time-limited set of marketing activities that works towards a defined goal, such as announcing a new product, filling a workshop or winning applicants. OpenStax describes the method of using various forms of promotion to send the same message to the target audience as integrated marketing communications. A campaign therefore has one core message that appears in several channels.

## Questions every plan answers

Write the answers in this order (a checklist from this course, based on the plan elements in OpenStax): What do we want to achieve, and by when? Who is the target group? What is the one core message? Which channels reach this group? What will it cost? How will we know whether it worked?

## Action programme: what, when, who, cost

OpenStax says strategies should be translated into specific action programs that show what is to be done, when, by whom and at what cost, with start, review and completion dates. In practice this is a table with one row per activity. Without a name next to each row, nothing happens on time.

## A budget as a simple plan

The German Wikipedia article on Budgetierung defines a budget as a plan expressed in value terms for a defined period and says budgeting means drawing up this plan and later checking against it. For a first campaign: list every cost (content creation, paid reach, tools, external help, printing), add a reserve for surprises, and compare the total to the money you actually have. If it does not fit, remove activities rather than hoping.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented organisation. A community college in Kassel wants 30 sign-ups for an evening course on spreadsheets. The core message: "Learn spreadsheets in four evenings, no prior knowledge needed." Channels: the website, an email to former participants (who agreed to receive course news), and posters in the library. The action programme lists the poster design (assistant, by week 1), the email (course coordinator, week 2) and a review after two weeks. The budget lists printing and a small reserve, and the total is checked against the money approved.

## Key takeaways

- A campaign is a time-limited activity set towards one goal with one core message.
- Start from goal and target group, then choose channels.
- Every activity needs a date, an owner and a cost.
- A budget is a value-based plan that you check against later.
- Add a reserve, and cut activities if the total does not fit.
