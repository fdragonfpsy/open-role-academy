## Why this matters

Social media is where many people meet organisations in public: they comment, share, complain and ask questions. A social media presence is therefore not a poster that you hang up once. It is an ongoing conversation that you can plan but not fully control.

## What social media marketing is

OpenStax defines social media marketing as using social media platforms to deliver content that drives engagement with your brand. The German Wikipedia article adds that the target groups are not only customers: potential employees, journalists, bloggers and the public are addressed as well. Organic activity means the posts and replies your page publishes without paying for reach. Paid activity means sponsored content for which you pay the platform. Both need content and both need someone who reads the reactions.

## Advantages and limits

OpenStax lists these advantages: social media is highly targeted, allows engagement with and among users, can send visitors to your website, and is easily measured. It also names the main disadvantages. Brands do not have complete control over the message, because users can write critical comments freely, and a good presence needs continuous time and money for content and for monitoring and answering comments.

## Choosing a platform for a target group

Start with the people, not the tool. Ask where your target group already spends time and what they expect there. A professional network suits business-to-business topics, a visual platform suits products that look good in pictures, a video platform suits explanations. Being present on few platforms with regular activity is better than a neglected profile everywhere. Platform popularity changes quickly, so check current usage for your market instead of relying on old statistics.

## Posting rhythm and basic engagement

Write down a simple plan: which platform, how often, which topics, who publishes. A content calendar for four weeks is enough to start. Answer questions and comments within a time you can keep, say so on the profile if replies are only given on weekdays, and keep the tone you defined in your brand voice.

## Worked example

Illustration only; invented company. A small bicycle repair shop in Leipzig wants more customers from the neighbourhood. Its target group is commuters between 25 and 45. The owner picks one visual platform and one local community group instead of four channels. The plan: two posts per week (a repair tip, a before and after picture), one weekly check of comments on Monday morning, and a fixed reply to the question "How long does a repair take?". After a month the owner counts which posts led to questions or visits and drops the topics that did not.

## Key takeaways

- Social media marketing means delivering content that creates engagement, organically or paid.
- It is targeted and measurable, but you do not control what users say.
- Choose platforms by where your target group is and what you can maintain.
- A small written plan with rhythm, topics and owner beats spontaneous posting.
- Reading and answering reactions is part of the work, not an extra.
