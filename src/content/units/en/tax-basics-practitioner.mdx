## Why this matters

You now know the VAT chain. In daily work you apply it: you split invoices into net amount and tax, post them correctly, notice when a transaction crosses a border and make sure the company meets its reporting duties. This unit stays at concept level for Germany and the EU. It contains no rates, thresholds, deadlines or section numbers; look these up in current official sources or ask your tax advisor.

## Net, tax and gross

The **net amount** is the price without VAT, the **gross amount** is the price including VAT. For a business the tax is not income or expense in the usual sense: output tax collected is owed to the tax authority, and input tax paid is a claim against it. This is why bookkeeping systems keep separate accounts for output tax and input tax, and why the profit and loss account normally shows net figures. Mixing up net and gross is one of the most common sources of errors.

## VAT in the bookkeeping flow

Conceptually the flow is:

1. **Purchase invoice:** post the net amount to the expense or asset account and the tax to input tax. Check that the invoice is proper, because the deduction depends on it.
2. **Sales invoice:** post the net amount as revenue and the tax as output tax.
3. **Period end:** the difference between output tax and input tax is reported to the tax authority and paid (or refunded). Businesses calculate the tax themselves and report it electronically in advance returns; after the year ends, an annual VAT return follows (German article on the VAT law). Which period applies to your company and when payment is due depends on current rules: check with the Finanzamt or your advisor.

## Domestic, EU and third-country

- **Domestic:** supplier charges German VAT, customer deducts it as input tax.
- **Within the EU:** the English article says VAT is mainly taxed where the goods arrive. Under the German rules, a cross-border delivery between businesses in two EU states can be an exempt intra-Community supply in the state where transport begins. The recipient taxes the acquisition in their own country and can usually claim the same tax back as input tax. The supplier must be able to prove the conditions, typically a valid VAT identification number of the customer and evidence that the goods really moved. Such supplies are also reported in a summary report to the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern.
- **Third countries (outside the EU):** exports are typically zero-rated and imports generally bear VAT (English article). The exact handling is a specialist topic.

## Business tax types, by name

VAT is only one of several taxes. By name only: the **Gewerbesteuer** is a municipal tax on the earnings of commercial businesses; municipalities set a multiplier (Hebesatz) that determines the final tax. Corporations are liable because of their legal form, sole proprietors and partnerships to the extent they run a trade, whereas freelancers are not covered. Its starting point is the profit calculated under income or corporate tax rules, with certain additions and reductions. Income tax and corporate tax are the profit-based taxes of individuals and corporations. Details are for the tax advisor.

## When to ask

The Steuerberater is a professional who advises on tax matters, prepares tax returns and represents clients before the authorities. Involve one when a transaction does not fit the standard pattern.

## Worked example

Invented amounts, no real rate. Your company buys goods for a net 500 with input tax of 100 and sells them for a net 800 with output tax of 160. Revenue 800, cost 500: the net profit effect is 300. VAT effect: output tax 160 minus input tax 100 gives 60 to pay to the authority. The 60 does not reduce profit: it is the tax the customer paid and the company passes on. If a supplier from another EU state delivers instead, no domestic tax is charged on the invoice, and the company must check how the acquisition is taxed and reported.

## Key takeaways

- Net excludes tax, gross includes it; book output tax and input tax separately.
- The amount payable is output tax minus input tax, reported in periodic returns; check current deadlines with the authority.
- Intra-EU supplies between businesses are handled differently from domestic ones and need proof and reporting.
- Gewerbesteuer, income tax and corporate tax are other business-related taxes; know them by name and purpose.
- Ask a tax advisor for unusual transactions.
